# Key insights

- 3,866 crimes reported 1 Jan - 26 Feb 2025 (~68/day); 10.9% violent, 89.1% property.
- Theft/other + theft from auto + motor vehicle theft = 85% of all crimes.
- Ward 5 has the most crimes (788); Ward 8 has the highest violent share (24%) and the most gun incidents (72).
- Ward 3 is safest: 275 crimes, 3% violent.
- Evening is the busiest shift (1,663); midnight has the highest violent share (23%).
- Guns were used in 284 incidents (7.3%); knives in 59.
- Feb daily rate is 70 vs Jan 66.
